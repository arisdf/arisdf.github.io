In 2549 AD, astronaut James Bob set foot on the Utopia Planitia on Mars.

Father Earth's voice came through the helmet newsletter: "Remember to send back your diary every day."

He crouched down, his gloves brushing away the dust and sand, revealing a pale green crystal cluster embedded in the basalt - never seen in any detection report. His teammate called him back to the cabin, but he quietly put it in his inner pocket.

As scientific observations confirmed that Mars might be a dead silence, the story began to shift towards mourning the demise of civilization and reflection on human nature.
