Deep in the core of the sun lives a creature invisible to the naked eye - the Flame Spirit. They are not flames but "the precursors of light". Each flame spirit is like a small ball of thinking gold dust, playing, sleeping, being born and dissipating in the heat wave of nuclear fusion.

The youngest flame spirit is called Gold Burning. It always loves the light sphere layer on the surface of the past: "I also want to become a beam of light and go see the world below!"

Old Flame Spirit warned it, "Once you turn into light and leave the sun, you will forget that this is home and can never come back."

Gold Burning wouldn't listen. At the moment of a flare, it leaped out along the stream of photons. After an eight-eight-second journey through the dark interstellar space, it finally reached a blue-green planet and landed on the petal of a dandelion.

Dandelions are tinted with a faint golden light. When the wind blows, the seeds, carrying golden embers, fly across fields, rivers and cities.

Gold Burning saw ants carrying grain, butterflies breaking out of their cocoons, and children chasing bubbles and laughing... It suddenly understood what Old Flame Spirit had said - it had truly forgotten the appearance of the core of the sun, only remembering a warm and vague feeling.
